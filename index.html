Key Insights
Growth

Sessions grew 50% from Jan to May

The Hub has steadily climbed from 1,716 sessions in January to 2,570 in May — a 50% increase in five months. Slackbot content entering the Featured Tools category in March–April was the primary catalyst.

Watch

May bounce surge is a yellow flag

Bounce sessions more than doubled in May (478 → 961), driven almost entirely by the Slackbot guide (50 → 193 bounces). In GA4, bounces are sub-10-second sessions — this traffic spike is likely low-intent or from a mismatched referral source.

Momentum

Clicks nearly doubled since January

Total clicks grew from 135 in January to 277 in May (+105%). This outpaces session growth, meaning visitors are clicking more per visit — a sign of improving content engagement quality.

Decline

New User Onboarding sliding since March

Workshop 101 peaked in March (570 sessions) and has dropped each month since: 570 → 199 → 159. It was the #1 article site-wide in Q1 and is now #4. Worth investigating whether the on-demand experience still resonates.

Stable

Salesforce in Slack: quiet, consistent growth

The SF Channels Implementation Guide has grown every month (Jan: 252 → May: 246 sessions). Across all four SF in Slack articles, sessions rose 22% Apr→May with strong click engagement. Low bounce, high intent.

Agentforce

Agentforce traffic inconsistent despite expansion

Category peaked in February (175 sessions on Launching Agentforce alone) then dropped back. More localized pages appeared in Mar–May but didn't add volume. Total category sessions have barely moved since Jan.

Recommendations
1

Investigate the Slackbot guide traffic source

Sessions doubled but bounce nearly quadrupled — pull the referral breakdown for that article specifically. If a single source is sending mismatched traffic, consider whether the content or CTA needs to be updated for that audience.

2

Refresh Workshop 101 or add a follow-on

NUO dropped 72% from its March peak. The on-demand webinar format may be losing relevance. Consider either refreshing the content, adding a second NUO resource, or testing a different format (e.g., resource guide vs. webinar).

3

Double down on Salesforce in Slack content

This category has the best engagement profile — consistent sessions, growing clicks, low bounce. It's likely serving high-intent admins. Adding a second or third piece (e.g., a troubleshooting guide or use-case spotlight) could capture more of this audience.

4

Audit Agentforce content strategy

Category has grown in page count (localized versions) but not in sessions. Volume is dominated by the collection page, not resource guides. Consider whether the right content types and CTAs are in place to drive deeper engagement.
